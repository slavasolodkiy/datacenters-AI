# Signal card 03 — Google Cloud London outage and Revolut vs limited local AI failover

- Event date: 20 Oct 2026
- Sources: [UptimeRobot report](https://uptimerobot.com/knowledge-hub/reports-research/google-cloud-london-outage-revolut/) · [Irish News: Revolut services down as Google Cloud apologises](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/revolut-services-down-as-google-cloud-apologises-for-outage-XCDIUTFFRVMY3GEVX5LIBV2NNA/) · [Irish News: banks and courts affected](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/banks-and-courts-affected-by-disruption-as-google-apologises-for-cloud-outage-NGVJFIJO7NK47DX5KKSFPTUGNQ/)
- What happened: a Google Cloud outage in London disrupted Revolut services and other UK organisations, including banks.
- Overlap with the 2025 proposal: concentration risk in centralized cloud regions is one argument for distributed compute.
- Difference and limit: local or distributed AI nodes cannot stand in for a bank's ledger, card processing or core systems. At most they could keep limited, non-critical AI tasks (for example internal document processing) running in a degraded mode. That is a hypothesis, not a tested capability.
- What transfers: a narrowly scoped "degraded-mode AI" scenario for operational-resilience discussions, not a replacement for cloud.
- Proposal date for comparison: 11 Oct 2025 (GitHub release v12.10.2025).
