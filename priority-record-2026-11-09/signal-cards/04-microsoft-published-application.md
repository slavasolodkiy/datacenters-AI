# Signal card 04 — Microsoft: a published patent application, not a granted patent or a deployed service

- Dates: filed 30 Apr 2025 · published 5 Nov 2026 (as reported)
- Record: US 2026/0349820 A1, "Opportunistic Micro-training on Edge Devices Using Spare GPU Utilization" — [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260349820A1/en). The page did not load during compilation of this record; number, title and dates were not independently re-verified here. Press coverage: [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/microsoft-ai-use-your-gpu/) (not fetched).
- What it is, as reported: using spare GPU capacity on edge devices for AI training, with device owners compensated.
- Overlap with the 2025 proposal: monetising spare consumer GPU capacity for AI work.
- Difference: the filing date (30 Apr 2025) is earlier than this proposal's public date (11 Oct 2025), so this record makes no "first" claim. A published application is not a granted patent and not evidence of a launched Microsoft service. The proposal here emphasises owner mandates (who may use the device, when, for what task class, with revocation) and payout compliance, which the public reporting does not describe.
- Proposal date for comparison: 11 Oct 2025 (GitHub release v12.10.2025).
