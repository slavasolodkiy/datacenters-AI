# Signal card 01 — Starlink Community Host (host sells passes)

- Event date: 1 Oct 2026 (launch reported; rolling out by area)
- Source: [Tesla North, 1 Oct 2026](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/01/starlink-community-host-program-passes/); also [Broadband Breakfast](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/starlink-opens-beta-for-community-internet-hosts/), [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4828/you-can-now-earn-money-by-sharing-your-starlink-connection)
- What it is: a host shares one Starlink kit with nearby users, who buy hour, day, week or month passes. The host earns income from connections; Starlink handles payments, user access and connectivity. Pass pricing was not announced in the source.
- Overlap with the 2025 proposal: an owner monetises spare capacity of a device they already own, while the platform runs onboarding and settlement.
- Difference: bandwidth, not compute. No AI workloads, no work verification problem of the kind compute needs.
- What transfers: the platform-run settlement model ("host earns, platform collects and pays") is the part a compute network still has to solve.
- Proposal date for comparison: 11 Oct 2025 (GitHub release v12.10.2025).
